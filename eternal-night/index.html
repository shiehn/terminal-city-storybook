Chapter 13

Eternal Night

Some placeshold nightbefore the sun is gone.A sealed doorway.Old letteringunder newer rain.A blanket foldedaway from the wet.A cup kept dry.A person arranginga few things carefullybeneath an overhang.Traffic passes.Eyes forward.The camera comes up.Then down.No photograph.No note.Nothing to solve.Stayed across the streetuntil the rain softened.Another person stopped too.No rescue.No spectacle.Just two peoplerefusing to look away.For a little whilethe darkness had company.

Terminal City · Storybook v0.14.0 · built 2026-10-15
Terminal City is an original fictional mythology inspired by Vancouver’s geography, weather, wildlife, infrastructure, art and electronic music. It makes no claim to historical, Indigenous or religious tradition. Real places are used as settings; the hidden system layered across them is invented.
Made by Steve Hiehn, a software developer and electronic musician based in Vancouver, Canada. All music was created using a custom DAW, Signals & Sorcery.
