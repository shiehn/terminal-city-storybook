Chapter 10

The Sixth Hour

Usuallythe music is heardthrough walls.Tonightthe wall is behind.Six hoursafter the first beat,the room is still moving.Moths crossone steady bulb.Huge wings on brick.A second lightcomes on.The rhythm breaks.Someone switches it off.The room finds itself again.Hands on drums.Hands on machines.Someone laughingwithout being heard.Strangers holdingone thing togetherwithout discussing how.No conductor.No centre.No audienceseparate from it.For several minutesthe whole roommoves like one animal.The doorway feelsvery far away.Not trapped.Held.And underneath the noise,the impossible thought:this may be whatall those wires,birds,walls,trainsand late doorswere trying to keep alive.

Terminal City · Storybook v0.13.0 · built 2026-10-14
Terminal City is an original fictional mythology inspired by Vancouver’s geography, weather, wildlife, infrastructure, art and electronic music. It makes no claim to historical, Indigenous or religious tradition. Real places are used as settings; the hidden system layered across them is invented.
Made by Steve Hiehn, a software developer and electronic musician based in Vancouver, Canada. All music was created using a custom DAW, Signals & Sorcery.
