The Far Shore

ReceiveNorth Vancouver140 BPM
The story

Across Burrard Inlet, on the lower slopes of the North Shore, the apartment lights come on at night. Most nights they are only lights. But sometimes they arrange themselves into long, flowing shapes, like a script written in a language nobody in Terminal City speaks.

The city cannot read it, and yet it can act on it. The Probes send unknown information across the water to be translated here, and the Wire Choir rebroadcasts whatever comes back. The Beyond often flickers on the skyline before a new message appears. Some residents of the Far Shore can read the lights. None of them admit it.

There is one rule that never changes: the message can only be read from the south side of the water. Cross over and it is just apartments again. Far shore. Light by light. Read the night. Send it back.
