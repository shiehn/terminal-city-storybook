Chapter 09

Sunset

Brushes washed.Cans stacked.Crows crossing eastthrough copper light.A small devicechanges handsbehind a building.On the Drive,yesterday's paint cansbecome drums.Someone stops.Then another.A door opens.Music movesinto the street.People who were heading homedo not.A painterwith colour still on one sleeveleans against the walland listens.Nothing appears broken.Nobody appears to be working.And still,this feels likethe thing all the work was for.The thought arrives whole:maybe making thingsis not decorationon top of the city.Maybe it iswhat the city is tryingto remain capable of.

Terminal City · Storybook v0.14.0 · built 2026-10-15
Terminal City is an original fictional mythology inspired by Vancouver’s geography, weather, wildlife, infrastructure, art and electronic music. It makes no claim to historical, Indigenous or religious tradition. Real places are used as settings; the hidden system layered across them is invented.
Made by Steve Hiehn, a software developer and electronic musician based in Vancouver, Canada. All music was created using a custom DAW, Signals & Sorcery.
