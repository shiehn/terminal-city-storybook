The Probes

Wind in the microphone.Rigging.Engines.Gulls.A ship movingbetween the cranes.Two people tappingdifferent versionsof the same rhythm.Neither agrees.Both sound right.Playback:mostly wind.Then something underneath.Low.Too widefor the little speaker.A crane turns inland.Stopped recording.Watched instead.Another cranestayed facing the water.For a momentthe whole harbourlooked less like a portand more likesomething listening.The thought felt absurd.The feeling did not.

Terminal City · Storybook v0.13.0 · built 2026-10-10
Terminal City is an original fictional mythology inspired by Vancouver’s geography, weather, wildlife, infrastructure, art and electronic music. It makes no claim to historical, Indigenous or religious tradition. Real places are used as settings; the hidden system layered across them is invented.
Made by Steve Hiehn, a software developer and electronic musician based in Vancouver, Canada. All music was created using a custom DAW, Signals & Sorcery.
