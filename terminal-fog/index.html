Terminal Fog

Deep NightHarbour / citywide160 BPM
The story

When the after-hours rooms empty and the last freight has cleared, the fog comes in from the water. The Outer Circuit disappears first. Then downtown. Then the rails. Then East Van. The last thing you can see, before the whole city is gone, is Trout Lake.

Inside the fog, Terminal City is taken apart and put back together, just differently enough to survive another day. A building might be missing when the fog lifts. A street might be a little shorter. The damage from the Sixth Hour is covered over. Sometimes the fog comes up out of the Endless Stair instead of in from the sea.

Then the fog thins, Trout Lake appears first, the water goes still, and the Morning Meeting begins again. The city never comes back exactly the same, and that is how it survives. Everything has returned so far. City ends. White line. No horizon. Start again.
