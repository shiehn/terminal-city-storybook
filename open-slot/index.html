The Open Slot

ResetUnassigned170 BPM
The story

Terminal City keeps one slot in its daily cycle empty on purpose. It has no place, no creature, no rule about wires or weather. It is a blank page at the end of the book, and the city likes it that way.

Whatever earns the slot will be something Terminal City already does, every day, that nobody has thought to name yet. It might be something small. It might be something you have walked past a hundred times. Until then, the slot stays open, and the cycle rolls from Terminal Fog straight back to the Morning Meeting.
